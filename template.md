# Meeting Notes Skeleton

This document serves as the standard skeleton for PhD supervision meeting notes. For each meeting, create a copy of this file and name it using the format YYYYMMDD.md. The student is responsible for completing the pre-meeting sections in advance, while meeting notes, advisor feedback, and action items are completed during or after the meeting. Each file should be treated as a persistent record of progress, decisions, and agreed next steps, and will be used to prepare for subsequent meetings.

---

## 1. Pre-Meeting Preparation (Student – to be completed *before* the meeting)

### 1.1 Progress Since Last Meeting
- Summary of work completed
- Key results (figures, experiments, proofs, drafts, etc.)
- Links to code, papers, or documents (if applicable)

### 1.2 Challenges and Open Issues
- Technical difficulties
- Conceptual questions
- Blockers (data, resources, feedback delays, etc.)

### 1.3 Agenda and Questions for Advisor
- Topics the student would like to discuss
- Specific questions requiring feedback or decisions

---

## 2. Meeting Notes (During the Meeting)

### 2.1 Discussion Summary
- Main points discussed
- Clarifications provided
- Alternatives or approaches considered

### 2.2 Decisions Made
- Agreed technical or research directions
- Scope adjustments
- Deadlines or priority changes

### 2.3 References Mentioned
- Papers, books, datasets, tools, or people mentioned during discussion

---

## 3. Administrative Notes (Optional)
- Coursework, exams, committee, funding, travel, etc.

---

## 4. Advisor Feedback (Advisor – during or after the meeting)

### 4.1 Scientific / Technical Feedback
- Strengths of current work
- Issues to address
- Suggestions for improvement or alternative approaches

### 4.2 Writing / Presentation Feedback
- Structure, clarity, or rigor
- Figures, proofs, experiments, or narrative issues

### 4.3 Strategic Guidance
- Long-term direction
- Publication strategy
- Career or training advice (if applicable)

---
